About

Email GitHub Google Scholar ResearchGate CV ↓

I am a PhD candidate at the Jackson School of Geosciences, University of Texas at Austin, working within the Gulf Coast Carbon Center (GCCC) at the Bureau of Economic Geology, co-advised by Dr. Seyyed A. Hosseini and Dr. Larry W. Lake. I am currently a research intern at Los Alamos National Laboratory (LANL), where my work focuses on techno-economic analysis of critical mineral recovery pathways.

My research focuses on coupled subsurface flow and geomechanics for carbon capture and storage (CCS), developing simulation workflows that are accurate enough for regulatory defensibility and fast enough for uncertainty campaigns. Current work spans boundary condition methods for truncated CCS domains, the Noordbergum effect as a monitoring diagnostic, and fault integrity under evolving pressure and stress fields.

Before UT Austin, I was a reservoir engineer at Chiang Mai University's CCS Research Centre in Thailand. I hold an M.Sc. in Petroleum Engineering from Chulalongkorn University (Thailand) and a B.Sc. from Universitas Islam Riau (Indonesia).

Research

Reservoir Simulation

Boundary Conditions for Truncated CCS Domains

Storage-conserving boundary-modifier workflows for reduced-domain reservoir models, improving pressure-footprint and AoR fidelity while enabling high-throughput uncertainty campaigns.

Geomechanics · Monitoring

Noordbergum Effect as a CCS Monitoring Diagnostic

Layered poroelastic framework to separate mechanical-coupling signatures from leakage-like pressure transients, sharpening sensor placement and operational decision triggers.

Fault Integrity · Risk

Fault Integrity Under Coupled Pressure and Stress Evolution

Fault-integrity screening coupling flow outputs with slip tendency and stress rotation across injection controls and fault-property uncertainty to define safe operating envelopes.

Critical Minerals · LANL

Techno-Economic Analysis of Critical Mineral Recovery

Techno-economic assessment of critical mineral extraction pathways, evaluating cost structures, supply chain feasibility, and policy implications to support domestic energy transition goals.
